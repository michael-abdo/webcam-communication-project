Intent Attribution Assessment

Read each scenario carefully and choose the explanation that best describes why you think the person acted that way.

Consider their likely intentions and motivations.

Scenario 1
You sent an important work email to a colleague 3 days ago asking for their input on a project deadline. They haven't responded yet, and you know they usually check email regularly. The deadline is approaching and their input would be very helpful.
Why do you think your colleague hasn't responded to your email?
How confident are you in this assessment?
Scenario 2
During a team meeting, you're presenting an idea that you've worked hard on. A colleague interrupts you mid-sentence to point out a potential problem with your proposal. They speak in a direct tone and continue to ask several challenging questions about your approach.
Why do you think your colleague interrupted and challenged your idea?
How confident are you in this assessment?
