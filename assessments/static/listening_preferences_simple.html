Listening Preferences Assessment

This assessment explores your preferred listening style when others share problems or concerns with you.

For each scenario, choose the response that best reflects what you would naturally want to do.

Scenario 1: Friend's Work Stress
Your close friend calls you feeling overwhelmed and stressed about their workload. They say: "I have three major projects due this week, my boss keeps adding more tasks, and I don't know how I'm going to get everything done. I'm feeling really anxious and can't sleep well."
What would be your most natural response?
Scenario 2: Partner's Family Conflict
Your romantic partner is upset about a disagreement with their family. They share: "My parents are pressuring me to visit for the holidays, but I really don't want to deal with all the family drama right now. They're making me feel guilty for wanting some space, and I'm torn between what I need and what they expect."
What would be your most natural response?
Scenario 3: Colleague's Career Disappointment
A colleague you're friendly with mentions during lunch: "I just found out I didn't get the promotion I've been working toward for the past year. They gave it to someone with less experience. I'm questioning whether I'm cut out for this career path and wondering if I should start looking elsewhere."
What would be your most natural response?
