Understanding Personalized Assessment Calibration

🤔 What is Baseline Capture?

Problem: Everyone has different "normal" facial expressions and speaking patterns. Current systems use generic thresholds that might incorrectly flag natural behaviors as "unusual".

Solution: Before any assessment, we capture 10 seconds of your neutral face and 15 seconds of your normal speech. This creates a personalized baseline so the system compares you against yourself, not everyone else.

❌ Without Baseline (Generic)

"Your eyes are 30% closed - you seem fatigued!"

But maybe this person naturally has slightly closed eyes...

✅ With Baseline (Personalized)

"Your eyes are normally 25% closed, now they're 30% - slight increase in fatigue."

Much more accurate assessment!

📋 Step 1: Neutral Face Baseline (10 seconds)
Instructions for User:
  • Sit comfortably in front of your camera
  • Look directly at the camera with a neutral expression
  • Don't smile, frown, or make any expressions
  • Keep your eyes naturally open (don't force them wide or squint)
  • Stay still for 10 seconds
10

Real-time Quality Feedback:

Face Confidence: 0.6 (Need >0.8) Lighting: Poor

💡 The system checks if your face is clearly visible with confidence >0.8

🎤 Step 2: Speech Baseline (15 seconds)
Instructions for User:
  • Read this sentence in your normal speaking voice:
  • "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This sentence helps calibrate my normal speech patterns."
  • Then count from 1 to 20 in your regular pace
  • Speak clearly but don't change your natural tone or volume
15

Real-time Audio Quality:

Audio SNR: 15.2 dB (Need >20 dB) Background Noise: High

🔊 The system checks if your voice is clear with Signal-to-Noise ratio >20dB

📊 What Gets Captured & Stored

Face Baseline Data:

  • 468 facial landmark positions
  • Average eye openness percentage
  • Natural blink rate
  • Neutral expression patterns
  • Head pose and gaze direction

Speech Baseline Data:

  • Average pitch and tone
  • Natural speaking rate
  • Voice intensity levels
  • Pause patterns
  • Audio quality metrics
🔒 Privacy: You can choose to store only the processed baseline metrics (default) or include the raw 25-second video. All data is encrypted and you can delete it immediately after use.
⚡ How This Improves Assessments

After baseline capture, during any assessment:

  • Real-time Comparison: Your current facial expressions and speech are compared to YOUR baseline, not generic thresholds
  • Personalized Coaching: "You seem more tired than usual" vs "You seem tired compared to everyone"
  • Accurate Detection: Reduces false positives for people with naturally expressive faces or unique speech patterns
  • Better Insights: Tracks how YOU change over time, not how you compare to others
